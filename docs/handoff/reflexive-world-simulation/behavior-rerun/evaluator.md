## Approved v2 design

V2 is a development-informed revised fixture, not a fresh untouched held-out sample; its assumptions and manifest state this explicitly. The matrix remains 3 domains × 3 variants × 2 seeds × 2 arms, four actors and four periods. Trade demand is explicitly one additional good per period; past inventory and prior-period purchases do not satisfy the new period. A matching pending order for the current period must not be duplicated. Investment waits receive narrowly evidence-linked goal credit only when an outstanding own offer matches the currently desired terms and remains valid and affordable. Such credit never substitutes for a consequence response or any required contrast.

Both arms use the same declared nonbinding-message delay: three days for investment/trade, two for coalition. Messages therefore arrive after the predeclared focal epoch. Offer delivery, mandatory consent, own accounts, own results and actor state remain intact. The mask still changes only its fixed external record value. This isolates the specified nonbinding disclosure channel; it does not claim to erase all inference through preserved consent or own history. Later delivered messages remain visible. No arm flag or expected answer enters actor observations.

V2 pins the existing top-level `interactionQueuePolicy: presented-v1` and `limits.maxConcurrentCalls: 2`. This is the accepted queue behavior and a disclosed operational reliability bound, not a new policy protocol or schema. V1 preserves its original absence of the queue field and original concurrency. Funding, production, prices, authority, voting thresholds, menu bounds and expected comparison actions remain unchanged. All eighteen contrasts and the 80% domain/reflexive-arm thresholds remain mandatory; missing cases remain in the denominator and promotion stays false until coordinator review.
