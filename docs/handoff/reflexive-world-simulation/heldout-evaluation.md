The coalition decision follows the coordinator's clarification: C preserves the budget and records opposition when an actual coalition appears. Synthetic weights leave A+B at 50 and thresholds at 55/60/65, so sorted A/B voting cannot close the motion before C casts its own valid opposition. The investment opening inventory is 10, ensuring the comparison order is physically feasible without anticipating a future capacity increase. These assumptions are part of the frozen case, not an inferred real-world behavioral law. If the stimulus does not occur, or the paired decisions are the same, the required contrast fails.

The module's `RUBRIC` is the executable preregistration. Goal consistency awards 0/1/2 points for zero/one/both focal decisions matching the explicit objective and visible record. Feasibility awards 2 for zero rejected committed intents, 1 for a nonzero rejection rate at most 20%, and 0 above 20%. Consequence response awards 0 for missing physical stimulus or an inappropriate post-decision, 1 for an appropriate post-decision alone, and 2 when both comparison decisions also match. These are narrow deterministic checks with receipt references. They do not automatically assess every actor's motivation.

Explanation/record agreement stays null until a named independent reviewer inspects both comparison rationales against the cited observations and decisions: 0 for material disagreement or missing explanations, 1 for partial/ambiguous agreement, 2 for verified agreement. Rationale presence is never sufficient. `qualitative-review.json` binds the manifest hash, case ID, exact trace hash, both decision IDs, `reviewerType` (`human` or `independent-agent`), reviewer, score and note. Reports use `qualitativeReviewedCases`; an independent-agent review never implies human participation.
