## Gaps found and fixed in the same session

1. **KIQs without data needs got no data, even when their question names a statistic.** In `us_inflation`, the planner gave needs only to the CPI and base-rate KIQs. Two v3 rules then excluded everything else:
   - Fallback seeding ran only when no KIQ had needs.
   - Catalog hints went only to data and base-rate KIQs.

   So the Fed-path KIQ, kind "actor", stored no policy rate and made no data call.

   Fix (`linear_research.py`):
   - Per-KIQ coverage. For each of a KIQ's first two own-words statistics (catalog topic), link a series the run already requested or stored, else prefetch its best match into a free slot.
   - Hints for every KIQ, from its own words, interleaved across statistics.
   - A planner sentence asking for needs on actor and policy KIQs about measured quantities.

   Test: `test_a_kiq_the_planner_left_without_needs_gets_the_series_its_own_words_name`. The two later runs, which had the planner change, gave needs to 3 of 4 and 4 of 4 KIQs.

2. **False positives in the QA check `data_numbers_consistent`.** It is advisory and never blocks publication.
   - "mid-2026" was read as minus 2026.
   - A question's own thresholds ("above 3.5% … below 2.5% within 15 months") counted as data claims.
   - So did a scenario's own weight ("跌破四（35%）").

   Fix: a hyphen joined to a word or digit is no sign; question and KIQ thresholds after a comparison word are ignored; a scenario weight next to its name is ignored. Every other number in a dataset-only sentence is still checked. The flags fell from 5 (`us_inflation`, `cn_gdp`) to 1 (`dc_power`), and that remaining flag is genuine: the writer's own "above roughly 5%".

   Test: `test_data_check_reads_hyphenated_years_and_the_questions_own_thresholds`.

3. **Node servers could not reach distant US-government hosts.** The live catalog smoke found 43 of 51 US Gov recipes failing with "fetch failed" (EIA, Treasury, USAspending).
   - Cause: Node 22 gives each address only 250 ms to connect (happy eyeballs), and from this network every address of api.eia.gov timed out. curl connected in about 1.5 s.
   - Fix: `data_mcp` passes `--network-family-autoselection-attempt-timeout=2500` to Node servers on Node versions that accept it (20.13+, 22.1+).
   - Result: US Gov re-smoked 51 of 51.

   Test: `test_node_servers_get_the_connect_attempt_flag_only_where_node_accepts_it`.

   The first `dc_power` attempt had synced the old `data_mcp.py`. It was stopped after about 33 s of planning and re-run on the fixed code; its directory is kept as `dc_power.aborted-stale-data-mcp`.

4. **Chart and label titles of US state series** read "Virginia, commercial sector — United States". A US state name now counts as naming the geography, in `data_sources.series_label` and its renderer mirror.

   Test: `test_a_us_state_series_title_needs_no_country_suffix`.
