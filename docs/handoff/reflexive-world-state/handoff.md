## 5) Findings, Decisions, Assumptions
- **Finding:** the previous "world state" was a scenario-share distribution evolved by one batched elicitation per round; the only inter-round feedback was a 900-character qualitative post digest. Agents never observed consequences, so the simulation measured discourse.
- **Finding:** `_InbandWorldEvolution` ordered updates by platform watermarks but was not a causal barrier: a fast platform could start round r+1 before round r was stepped. The coordinator's `begin_round(r+1)` now awaits the commit of r (barrier timeout `SIM_WORLD_BARRIER_TIMEOUT_S`, default 1800 s; a stalled platform is marked and the round commits with what was delivered).
- **Decision:** deciding actors make ONE structured decision per round that REPLACES their OASIS post call; the statement is published as a tagged `CREATE_POST` ManualAction on both platforms, so cost stays roughly neutral and every actor decides once per round. Actors whose decision failed or was invalid fall back to the organic post; holders post nothing but still take part in the reaction step. Alternative (second call on top of the OASIS post) rejected for cost and inconsistency.
- **Decision:** the arbiter call REPLACES the per-round scenario elicitation; scenario commitments come from the decisions themselves (each actor names the scenario its move pushes toward or abstains). Audience/sampled agents without a decision contribute no commitment.
- **Decision:** deterministic updates never fabricate numeric effects; variables move only through the validated arbiter output (clamped by `SIM_WORLD_MAX_CHANGE_RATIO`) or declared pending effects. When the arbiter is off or fails, facts hold and the round still commits.
- **Decision (changed during testing):** a commitment stays pending until due; it is honoured by acting (any non-hold move) in the round it falls due and lapses the round after otherwise. An arbiter "success" on the action no longer fulfils forward commitments (it only nudges reputation). Reason: the first loop run showed the pledge disappearing from the brief the moment the move succeeded.
- **Decision:** `agency_class` is inferred deterministically (dossier value → ontology value → type/role text with word-boundary matching); the ontology addendum asks the LLM for `agency_class` and `levers` on actor/collective types and normalisation fills gaps.
- **Decision:** world-model artifacts are per-run and rotate on rerun (`world_state.json`, `world_state_history.jsonl`, `world_actions.jsonl`, and the previously un-rotated `world_digest.jsonl`).
- **Assumption:** rounds complete within the barrier timeout. Falsification: covered only by the two-platform loop test (no timeout path exercised live); the timeout branch marks the missing platform stalled and continues — monitor in the live run.
- **Finding (live run):** the seed covered the research well — 24 numeric state variables (all `provenance=research`, with units and as-of dates), 7 actors with inferred classes (3 sovereign_government, 2 individual_leader, 1 firm, 1 legislature), 7 valenced relationships — but `documented_plans` and `red_lines` were empty for every actor: the v3 `actors.json` carries `goals`, `incentives`, `constraints`, `stated_vs_revealed`, `vulnerabilities`, never the `future_plans` / `likely_actions` / `red_lines` / `non_negotiables` keys the legacy branch of `world_model_seed` reads. Option menus therefore came from response options + class levers only; they were still varied (12 distinct non-hold option ids over 50 decisions).
- **Finding (live run):** the arbiter grows the world: it added 2 facts per applied round (14 in total: truce status, summit scheduling, Affiliates Rule in force, purchase framework status, MOFCOM suspension status…), 5 of them non-numeric. Numeric facts moved only when something binding happened (first change in round 3), through declared delayed effects and arbiter changes, always with a cause and inside the 0.5 change-ratio clamp.
- **Finding (live run):** provider content filtering is a real failure mode on political topics: Zhipu glm-5.3 returned HTTP 400 code 1301 (content filter) for 2 of 9 arbiter calls, 2 graph chunks, 2 market-relevance scorings, one report section (retry succeeded) and the report's binary-forecast extraction. The world loop degraded exactly as designed (see §7), the report did not (publish gate, unrelated to this branch).
- **Assumption:** roster/sampling differences between platforms are acceptable: the first platform to reach `decide(r)` fixes the round's deciding roster from its own active agents; the other platform publishes the same decisions and lets its extra sampled agents post organically.
